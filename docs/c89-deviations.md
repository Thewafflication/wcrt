# WCRT C89 deviations

**Content type:** Deviation register

**Status:** No accepted open project-code deviation

This register records intentional or unresolved differences between WCRT's
documented hosted C89/C90 surface and its selected standard baseline. It has no
open entries at present.

That status is not, by itself, an unqualified conformance claim. The
[requirements index](REQUIREMENTS.md), implementation-defined behavior,
platform support records, and passing test evidence together define the
supported contract. A newly discovered deviation must be recorded here and
linked to its affected requirement until corrected or explicitly accepted.
